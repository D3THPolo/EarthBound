About the Brand

Rooted in Style

EarthBound is a lifestyle clothing brand grounded in nature and driven by authenticity. Born from a desire to blend modern streetwear with earthy tones and sustainable values, we bring you fashion that feels just as good as it looks.

Each piece in our collection tells a story — a story of freedom, earth, and expression. Our designs are minimal, functional, and made for everyone who walks their own path.
